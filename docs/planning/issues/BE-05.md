## Что сделать
Тренировки и событие `workout_completed`.

## Детали / спецификация
- `backend-fit-baddy/app/modules/workouts/models.py`: `workout_plans`, `workout_plan_exercises` (name, sets, reps, weight_kg, rest_seconds, order_index), `workout_sessions` (status in_progress|completed, started_at, completed_at, duration_seconds, xp_awarded, scheduled_id).
- `GET/POST /api/v1/workouts/plans`, `GET /workouts/plans/{id}`; принимает `title` или `name`.
- `POST /workouts/sessions {workout_plan_id|plan_id, scheduled_id?}` → 201 `in_progress`.
- `POST /workouts/sessions/{id}/complete {duration_seconds?}` → 200; повтор → 409; публикует `workout_completed {user_id, session_id, completed_at, duration_seconds}`.
- `GET /workouts/history` → `{items: Session[], total}`; `GET /workouts/sessions/{id}`.
